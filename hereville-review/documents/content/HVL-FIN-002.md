### PURPOSE

This analysis was prepared at the request of the Hereville Town Council to provide a fiscal comparison of current two-school operations against a consolidated single-school model. Figures represent best available estimates and are subject to revision pending final consolidation planning decisions.

### PROJECTED COSTS — CONSOLIDATED MODEL (NORTHSIDE ONLY)

The following projections assume closure of Southside Elementary and consolidation of all K–5 students at Northside Elementary. Northside's current enrollment of 287 plus Southside's 194 yields a combined enrollment of 481 students, exceeding Northside's stated capacity of 420.

#### Capacity Resolution Options

**Option A — Modular Classroom Addition**
Addition of three modular classroom units to Northside campus.
Estimated one-time cost: $187,000–$224,000
Northside would operate at modified capacity of 480 students.

**Option B — Redistricting with Enrollment Cap**
Redistrict a portion of current Southside attendance zone to reduce combined enrollment to 420 or below through natural attrition and boundary adjustment.
Estimated one-time cost: $12,000–$18,000 (redistricting study and notification)
Timeline: 18–24 months to full effect.

*The district has not adopted a preferred option. Both are reflected in the cost projection below as a range.*

### STAFFING IMPLICATIONS

Current combined instructional and support staffing across both schools: 41.5 FTE
Projected consolidated staffing requirement: 29.0–31.5 FTE
Estimated reduction: 10–12.5 FTE

Of the projected reductions, approximately 4–5 positions are expected to be filled through retirement attrition within the next 24 months. Remaining reductions would require either reassignment to other district positions (limited availability) or separation.

The school division employs no instructional staff under collective bargaining agreements. Severance obligations are governed by individual contracts and Virginia Code provisions for licensed educational employees.

---

### LIMITATIONS OF THIS ANALYSIS

This analysis does not account for:
- Potential loss of enrollment if consolidation prompts families to seek alternative schooling options
- Long-term disposition costs or revenue from the Southside building and property
- Community services currently operating from the Southside facility (see HVL-COM-003)
- Demographic or enrollment trend changes resulting from future economic development
