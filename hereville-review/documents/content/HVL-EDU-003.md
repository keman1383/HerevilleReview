### AGE DISTRIBUTION

| Age Group | 1990 | 2023 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 18 | 28.4% | 20.1% | -8.3 pts |
| 18–34 | 22.1% | 16.8% | -5.3 pts |
| 35–54 | 26.8% | 24.4% | -2.4 pts |
| 55–64 | 10.4% | 16.2% | +5.8 pts |
| 65 and over | 12.3% | 22.5% | +10.2 pts |

> *Hereville's population is aging significantly. The share of residents under 18 has declined from 28.4% to 20.1% since 1990, consistent with declining school enrollment. The share of residents 65 and older has nearly doubled in the same period. This trend increases demand for senior services while reducing demand for educational services and is expected to continue.*
