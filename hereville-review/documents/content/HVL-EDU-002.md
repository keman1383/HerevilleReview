### TEN-YEAR DEMOGRAPHIC TREND — ELEMENTARY SCHOOLS COMBINED

| School Year | White % | Black % | Hispanic % | Other % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2014–15 | 61.4% | 26.8% | 9.4% | 2.4% |
| 2016–17 | 59.1% | 27.2% | 11.3% | 2.4% |
| 2018–19 | 57.6% | 27.8% | 12.4% | 2.2% |
| 2020–21 | 55.2% | 28.1% | 14.4% | 2.3% |
| 2022–23 | 53.8% | 28.6% | 15.3% | 2.3% |
| 2024–25 | 52.7% | 29.1% | 15.9% | 2.3% |

> *The district's Hispanic/Latino student population has grown from 9.4% to 15.9% of total enrollment over the past decade, representing the only demographic group showing consistent growth. This growth is concentrated in the Southside attendance zone, which encompasses the majority of Hereville's Spanish-speaking immigrant community.*

### ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE BY SCHOOL — VIRGINIA STANDARDS OF LEARNING (SOL)

Pass rates reflect percentage of students passing the Virginia SOL assessments in the most recent tested year (Spring 2024).

#### Reading SOL Pass Rates

| School | All Students | White | Black | Hispanic | FRPM Eligible |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Northside Elementary | 74.2% | 79.1% | 58.3% | 52.2% | 54.1% |
| Southside Elementary | 61.4% | 71.2% | 54.8% | 51.2% | 56.3% |
| Virginia State Average | 71.3% | 79.4% | 55.1% | 54.8% | 57.2% |

#### Math SOL Pass Rates

| School | All Students | White | Black | Hispanic | FRPM Eligible |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Northside Elementary | 71.8% | 76.4% | 54.1% | 49.6% | 51.8% |
| Southside Elementary | 58.9% | 67.3% | 51.4% | 48.8% | 53.4% |
| Virginia State Average | 68.7% | 76.1% | 50.3% | 52.1% | 54.6% |

> *When controlling for FRPM eligibility, the performance gap between Northside and Southside students narrows substantially in both reading and math. The overall school-level gap in pass rates is largely explained by differences in student population economic status rather than school-level instructional factors. This finding is consistent with the conclusions of the 2022 Virginia Department of Education Equity Audit (HVL-HIST-003).*
