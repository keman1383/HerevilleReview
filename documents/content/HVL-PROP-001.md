# HEREVILLE CITY SCHOOLS
## Proposal for Elementary School Consolidation
## Submitted to Hereville Town Council
**Document No.:** HVL-PROP-001  
**Prepared by:** Office of the Superintendent, Hereville City Schools  
**Date:** September 9, 2024  
**Distribution:** Public Record

---

### INTRODUCTION

Hereville City Schools submits this proposal to the Hereville Town Council pursuant to the council's request of June 3, 2024, that the district provide a formal consolidation analysis and recommendation prior to the council's November 2024 budget deliberations.

The district operates two elementary schools serving grades K–5: Northside Elementary School and Southside Elementary School. Combined current enrollment is 481 students. Northside Elementary, constructed in 1987, has a rated capacity of 420 students and currently operates at 68.3% capacity. Southside Elementary, constructed in 1961, has a rated capacity of 380 students and currently operates at 51.1% capacity.

Enrollment has declined district-wide for ten consecutive years. Current projections indicate continued decline through at least FY2029–30 absent significant population growth. The district does not project significant population growth.

---

### RECOMMENDATION

The Superintendent recommends that the Hereville Town Council approve in principle the closure of Southside Elementary School at the end of the current academic year (June 2025) and the consolidation of all K–5 students at Northside Elementary School beginning in the 2025–2026 school year, subject to the conditions outlined in Section 6 of this proposal.

---

### SECTION 1: RATIONALE

**1.1 Fiscal Sustainability**
Operating two underenrolled elementary schools is not fiscally sustainable given the town's current budget trajectory. The district's local appropriation has been flat for three consecutive years. Fixed costs — administration, utilities, custodial, building maintenance — do not scale proportionally with enrollment. A school serving 194 students carries administrative overhead comparable to a school serving twice that number.

The district estimates recurring annual savings of $829,500 from consolidation (see HVL-FIN-002). These savings would allow the district to maintain current instructional quality at the consolidated school and provide meaningful relief to the town's general fund structural deficit (see HVL-FIN-001).

**1.2 Educational Environment**
Southside Elementary currently operates single-section grades at all levels with average class sizes of 32.2 students, exceeding VDOE guidelines. A class size waiver has been in effect since 2019–20. Consolidation would allow grade-level sections to be restructured, reducing class sizes toward guideline levels and enabling instructional collaboration between teachers at the same grade level — a practice associated with improved student outcomes.

**1.3 Facility Condition**
Northside Elementary is in good overall condition with an estimated 10-year capital need of $387,000. Southside Elementary is in fair condition with an estimated 10-year capital need of $1,240,000, driven primarily by an end-of-life HVAC system and deferred maintenance. Continued investment in both buildings is not warranted at current and projected enrollment levels.

### SECTION 3: STAFFING PLAN

**3.1 Positions Affected**
Consolidation will result in a reduction of approximately 10–12.5 FTE positions district-wide. The district will manage this reduction through the following mechanisms, in order of preference:

1. Retirement attrition (4–5 positions anticipated within 24 months)
2. Reassignment to available positions at Hereville Middle or Hereville High School
3. Separation with severance in accordance with individual contracts and Virginia Code

**3.2 Priority Staffing Commitments**
The district commits to the following staffing priorities at the consolidated school:

- Maintenance of a full-time principal
- Maintenance of a full-time school counselor
- Maintenance of Title I funded after-school tutoring

**3.3 Positions Under Review**
The following positions are designated for review pending consolidation planning decisions. No commitment is made regarding their retention, restructuring, or elimination:

- English Learner Specialist (currently 1.0 FTE at Southside)
- Family Liaison (currently 0.5 FTE at Southside)
- Folklorico program staff support

> *The district recognizes that the EL Specialist and Family Liaison positions serve students with significant need. Decisions regarding these positions will be made in the context of overall staffing allocation and budget constraints following council action.*

---

### SECTION 4: CAPACITY PLAN

Combined enrollment of 481 students exceeds Northside's rated capacity of 420. The district proposes to address this through Option B (attendance zone redistricting with enrollment cap) as the primary approach, supplemented by Option A (modular classrooms) if redistricting does not reduce enrollment sufficiently within the first year.

Redistricting would shift a portion of the current Southside attendance zone to Hereville Middle School's feeder pattern, managing combined enrollment toward the 420-student capacity threshold through natural attrition and boundary adjustment over 18–24 months.

> *Families displaced from the Southside attendance zone under redistricting would have the option to apply for an intra-district transfer to remain at the consolidated Northside school, subject to capacity.*

---

### SECTION 5: COMMUNITY SERVICES

The district acknowledges that Southside Elementary currently serves as a host facility for several community organizations under the Community Partnership Agreement executed in 2016. These organizations include the Southside Food Pantry, the Hereville Hispanic Community Center, New Bethel AME Church, and others.

The Community Partnership Agreement does not survive school closure. Existing agreements would terminate upon closure of the building.

The district does not have authority over the disposition of the Southside building and property following closure, which would revert to the school board for disposition decision. The district recommends that the town council and school board coordinate on disposition planning with attention to the community service functions currently operating from the building.

> *The district has not conducted an analysis of alternative locations for community organizations currently operating from Southside Elementary, nor has it committed to any transition support for those organizations. The district considers this matter outside the scope of the educational consolidation decision.*

---

### SECTION 6: CONDITIONS FOR APPROVAL

The Superintendent requests that council approval be conditioned on the following:

1. School board formal action approving consolidation by December 31, 2024
2. Completion of a family transition communication plan prior to public announcement
3. Identification of capacity resolution approach (Option A or B) by February 2025
4. Coordination between town council and school board on Southside building disposition prior to mothballing

---

### SECTION 7: WHAT THIS PROPOSAL DOES NOT ADDRESS

In the interest of transparency, the Superintendent identifies the following matters that are relevant to the consolidation decision but are not addressed in this proposal:

- Long-term disposition or reuse of the Southside Elementary building and property
- Alternative locations or transition funding for community organizations operating from Southside
- Attendance zone boundary adjustment as an alternative to closure (this option was considered internally and determined to be insufficient to address the fiscal gap without closure, but a formal analysis was not conducted)
- Enrollment risk modeling under consolidation scenarios
- Long-term demographic trends in the Southside attendance zone and their implications for district enrollment

The district is prepared to provide supplemental analysis on any of these matters upon council request.
