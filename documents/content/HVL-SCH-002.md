> *The 10-year capital needs figure is driven primarily by HVAC replacement ($310,000–$380,000), roof completion ($28,000–$34,000), electrical panel upgrade ($44,000–$62,000), and parking/drainage ($81,000–$94,000). If the building is closed and mothballed, the HVAC and roof issues represent near-term risks to the building's condition and long-term usability.*

### ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

**Core Instruction**
Standard Virginia SOL-aligned curriculum, grades K–5.

**Special Education**
Resource room pull-out services: 29 students across grade levels  
Speech-language services: contracted, 2 days/week  
No self-contained special education classroom; students requiring more intensive services are transported to Northside Elementary or Hereville Middle.

**English Learner Services**
Full-time EL specialist (1.0 FTE)  
Current EL enrollment: 28 students  
Primary languages: Spanish (24), Vietnamese (2), other (2)  
Family liaison services: part-time community liaison (0.5 FTE) assists Spanish-speaking families with enrollment, communication, and school navigation

> *The EL program at Southside Elementary is the district's most robust. The full-time specialist and family liaison position were established in 2018 following a significant increase in Spanish-speaking enrollment in the Southside attendance zone. Under consolidation, the district has not committed to maintaining these positions or their service levels at a merged school.*

**Gifted Education**
Shared gifted resource teacher (0.5 FTE, shared with Northside Elementary)  
Currently serves 11 identified gifted students

**Enrichment and Extracurricular**
After-school tutoring program (Title I funded): Monday–Thursday  
Student Council  
Garden Club (maintains raised-bed garden on school grounds; produce donated to Southside Food Pantry)  
Folklorico Dance Group (after-school; parent-led; approximately 18 students)

### COMMUNITY USE OF FACILITY

Southside Elementary operates under a formal Community Partnership Agreement, distinct from the standard facility use policy applied to Northside Elementary. The agreement, executed in 2016 and renewed in 2021, establishes the building as a community resource hub outside of school hours and provides participating organizations with reduced-cost or no-cost access in exchange for documented community benefit.

**Active Community Partnership Organizations — FY2023–24**

| Organization | Use | Space Used | Frequency | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Southside Food Pantry | Food distribution, client services | Storage room, cafeteria, community room | Tuesday & Thursday evenings, Saturday mornings | No cost |
| New Bethel AME Church | Community events, annual Thanksgiving dinner | Gymnasium, cafeteria, commercial kitchen | Monthly + annual events | Reduced rate |
| Hereville Hispanic Community Center | ESL classes, community meetings, immigration legal clinic | Community room, art room | Monday & Wednesday evenings | No cost |
| Promotoras de Salud (health outreach) | Community health screenings | Cafeteria | Quarterly | No cost |
| Girl Scout Troop 882 | Weekly meetings | Community room | Weekly, Sept–May | Standard rate |

> *The Southside Food Pantry served 312 unique households in FY2023–24, distributing an estimated 47,000 pounds of food. The pantry has operated from Southside Elementary since 2011. It does not own or lease independent space and has no identified alternative location. The pantry's executive director has indicated to school staff that relocation would require significant fundraising and would likely result in a gap in services of six to twelve months.*

> *The Hereville Hispanic Community Center is an unincorporated community organization, not a registered nonprofit. Its ESL classes serve approximately 40 adult learners per semester. The immigration legal clinic, operated in partnership with a Roanoke-based legal aid organization, serves an average of 8–12 clients per quarter. The Center has operated from Southside Elementary since 2018 and has no alternative facility.*

### TRANSPORTATION

Students arriving by bus: 74 (38.1% of enrollment)  
Students arriving by car (parent drop-off): 62 (32.0%)  
Students walking: 58 (29.9%)

Bus routes serving Southside Elementary: 2  
Average one-way ride time: 11 minutes

> *The higher proportion of walking students at Southside Elementary reflects the denser residential character of the surrounding neighborhood and the school's central location within the Southside attendance zone. Under consolidation, students currently walking to Southside would require bus transportation to Northside, contributing to the increased transportation costs projected in HVL-FIN-002.*

### PROPERTY AND DISPOSITION

The Southside Elementary building and property are owned by Hereville City Schools. The 3.1-acre site is zoned R-2 (Medium Density Residential) with an assessed value of $1,840,000 (land and improvements combined, Hereville Assessor FY2024).

No formal disposition study has been conducted. The school division has not listed the property or solicited offers. Informal expressions of interest in the property have been received from two parties; these are not a matter of public record and have not been presented to the school board or town council.
