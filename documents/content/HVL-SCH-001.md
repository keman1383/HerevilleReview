### ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

**Core Instruction**
Standard Virginia SOL-aligned curriculum, grades K–5. All core instruction delivered by licensed classroom teachers.

**Special Education**
Self-contained special education classroom (grades 2–5): 1 section, 8 students  
Resource room pull-out services: 30 students across grade levels  
Speech-language services: contracted, 3 days/week  
Occupational therapy: contracted, 1 day/week

**English Learner Services**
Shared EL specialist (0.5 FTE, shared with Hereville Middle School)  
Current EL enrollment: 11 students  
Primary languages: Spanish (8), other (3)

**Gifted Education**
Part-time gifted resource teacher (0.5 FTE, shared with Southside Elementary)  
Currently serves 24 identified gifted students

**Enrichment and Extracurricular**
After-school tutoring program (Title I funded): Monday–Thursday  
Student Council  
Safety Patrol  
Battle of the Books (library program)  
Spring musical (PTA-funded)

### COMMUNITY USE OF FACILITY

Northside Elementary is available for community use outside of school hours under the Hereville City Schools Facility Use Policy. Community use requests are submitted to the district office and approved by the principal.

**Registered Community Users — FY2023–24**

| Organization | Use | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Hereville Youth Soccer Association | Practice/meetings | Sept–Nov, Mar–May |
| Cub Scout Pack 114 | Weekly meetings | Sept–May |
| Hereville Democratic Party | Precinct meetings | Occasional |
| Hereville Republican Party | Precinct meetings | Occasional |
| Private tutoring (2 individuals) | After-school use | Weekly |

> *Northside Elementary serves as a polling location for the North Hereville precinct during elections. This use is established by agreement with the Hereville Electoral Board and is not subject to the standard facility use approval process.*
