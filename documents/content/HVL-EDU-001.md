# HEREVILLE CITY SCHOOLS

**Document No.:** HVL-EDU-001  
**Prepared by:** Office of Planning & Assessment, Hereville City Schools  
**Date:** October 1, 2024  
**Distribution:** Public Record

---

### OVERVIEW

This report documents enrollment trends across Hereville City Schools for the ten-year period ending September 30, 2024. Data is drawn from official fall membership counts submitted annually to the Virginia Department of Education. Figures reflect students enrolled as of the official count date and do not include pre-kindergarten programs.

> *Projections do not account for potential enrollment impacts from school consolidation, boundary changes, or significant economic development activity.
